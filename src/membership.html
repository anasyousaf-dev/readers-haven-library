Membership Plans

Pick a plan that fits you. Join at the front desk with a valid ID card — your card is ready the same day.

Student

PKR 500 per year

  • Borrowing limit: 3 books at a time
  • Loan period: 14 days per book
  • Late return: Rs. 20 fine per book, per day

For school and university students with a valid student card.

Adult

PKR 1,000 per year

  • Borrowing limit: 5 books at a time
  • Loan period: 21 days per book
  • Late return: Rs. 30 fine per book, per day

For individual adult readers.

Family

PKR 1,800 per year

  • Borrowing limit: 8 books at a time
  • Loan period: 21 days per book
  • Late return: Rs. 30 fine per book, per day

One card for the whole family — up to 4 members can share it.

Good to know

Books can be renewed once if nobody else has reserved them. Lost or damaged books must be replaced or paid for at the current price.
