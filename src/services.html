Digital Services

More than books — these free services are open to all members during library hours.

Online Catalogue

Search the whole collection from home and reserve books before you visit.

  • Steps: open the catalogue, search by title or author, and reserve with your card number.
  • Available: 24 hours online; reserved books are held for 3 days.

E-books

Read e-books on your phone, tablet, or computer — no visit needed.

  • Steps: log in with your membership card number, pick a book, and download it.
  • Loan period: 14 days, then the book returns itself.

Study-Room Booking

Quiet rooms for group study and exam preparation.

  • Steps: book at the front desk or by phone, one day in advance.
  • Timings: Monday – Saturday, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM; 2 hours per booking.

Printing & Scanning

Print notes and scan documents at the service desk.

  • Charges: Rs. 10 per black-and-white page, Rs. 30 per colour page; scanning is free.
  • Timings: Monday – Saturday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM.

Free Wi-Fi

Free internet for members inside the library building.

  • Steps: ask the front desk for the daily password and connect to the Readers' Haven network.
  • Timings: all library opening hours.
